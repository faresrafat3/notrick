ASSERTED — stated as fact in the source, not yet verified by any run. Example: C0016 (niche law, RJ-001 L5).
ARGUED — supported by explicit reasoning in the source, still paper. Example: C0019 (verification-cost bankruptcy, cross-exam L57-59).
