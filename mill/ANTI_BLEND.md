# ANTI_BLEND — one-line purpose: rules that make blending impossible to pass as a verdict (op 0375).
1. A verdict names ONE doctrine, ONE object, and concrete discards with IDs — no "modalities".
2. Any sentence with "and / both / depending on / in its own way" between incompatible readings is deleted and logged.
3. The blend-temptation rows BT01-BT06 are a standing vocabulary of what to catch (BLEND_TEMPTATION.md).
4. A verdict that loses nothing is fake and must be re-done (LOST.md).
5. A verdict that "keeps all readings' strengths" is by definition a blend — delete it.
6. Scoring is mechanical (SCORES.md); emotional "feel right" reasons are inadmissible in verdicts.
7. The elected reading's killed alternatives must stay killed until KILL_REASON, and KILL_REASON must name the force from the files, not a mood.
8. Anti-blend is a checker duty: AUDITOR reviews every verdict-line for blend syntax before freeze.
