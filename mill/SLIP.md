1. burrow ≠ charter — the charter is the card; the burrow is the region it is allowed to act in (C0024, adversarial-audit L28-29).
2. gate ≠ MIC-23 — the gate is the criterion; MIC-23 only re-runs it and quotes output (mic-23 L7).
3. grid ≠ CONTEXT.md — the grid stores result squares addressed (DS-id, attempt); CONTEXT is a decision log (rcvm L24).
4. obituary ≠ retirement-trigger — an obituary is the death condition at birth (arc-protocol L7); a retirement trigger is a charter clause about one role's dissolution (ch-001 L26).
5. axis ≠ burrow — an axis is the dimension of an attempt; a burrow is the region an entity may act in (mic-31 L7 vs ch-001 L6).
6. receipt ≠ K-line — a receipt is per-line existence evidence; a K-line is the verdict tuple proving the square fits (pr-005 vs rcvm L52).
7. price ≠ result — under results-as-prices the result is the event, the price is the allocation it causes; sources never show the causation (G0005).
8. paper mode ≠ Phase A — paper mode is one brain rehearsing all seats; Phase A is Cline wearing MICs manually (proposal-cross-exam L50-52 vs mic-07 L20).
9. hash pin ≠ legality — a pin proves content only; a logged approved-docs moment proves approval (INTEGRITY L16).
10. boot gate ≠ zero day — the boot gate is per-reply standing; zero day is the first live run (AGENTS BOOT GATE vs ZERO-READINESS L2).
