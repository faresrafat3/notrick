# NICHE_TEST — one-line purpose: real breakthrough vs shallow-version tests for the niche (ops 0358-0359).
## Real-breakthrough test (0358)
A DS period closes: every claim has a receipt entry matching its gate re-run; three consecutive periods each catch at least one manufactured inconsistency and the record names it UNVERIFIED out loud — after the second period, two different claimers file the same kind of claim and a checker cannot tell which without re-running the close.
## Shallow-version test (0359)
The word "ledger" (or debit/reconcile/suspense) appears in a memo while no schema, no check, and no recorded entry exist for it. If the sentence survives removing the bookkeeping noun without changing behavior, it is the shallow version.
