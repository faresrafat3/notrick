Fragment: the Owner-queue as substitute verdict (D2 PRESSURE_RANK #3; K3; DS1) — twenty-plus rulings pending one human, accreting for days, doctrine growing around the stall.

## Why it does NOT fit the skeleton
1. The schema S=(C,R,K,M,F) states the condition of a RECORD. The queue is not a RECORD — it has no criterion, no receipt, no mark. It is a PROLONGED UNSET state, but the schema's M has no way to say "unset because a payer is absent" vs "unset because the test failed".
2. The discipline's move set (O1-O6) is per-RECORD. The queue is a scheduler problem — who pays K (COST) and when — and the skeleton deliberately has no scheduler (no role field; payer hides inside K). The doctrine refuses to model the queue's actual dynamics: an unbounded wait whose cost is paid by the SYSTEM (stalled fates) rather than by any RECORD.
3. The five-field discipline can flag each queue item UNVERIFIED individually (each has a named gap: "Owner line missing"). But the aggregate harm — doctrine accreting around the stall, the practiced object drifting (OFFICIAL_PRACTICE rows 2-6) — is a property of the CHAIN, not of any single RECORD. The schema has no chain-level state.

## What a force-fit would look like (and why it is refused)
- Inventing a sixth field "WAIT" or a chain-level MARK would express the queue — but it would smuggle a scheduler into a doctrine whose actual generating insight is per-record refusal. The queue's harm is real, and the discipline's blind spot to it is real; pretending the schema covers it is the kind of smoothness Day 2 was built to destroy.

## Honest statement
The machine generated here is a THEORY OF THE REFUSAL, not a theory of the stall. It prices checks, refuses unfunded claims, and catches fabrications — everything EX1 shows. What it cannot yet express: accumulating systemic cost of deferred judgment across a ledger. That gap is inherited from the verdict itself (R1 chose verification over R2's governance) and is recorded as the doctrine's known blind spot, to be attacked in Day 5 (red team, Q03 engine-does-not-generate) rather than patched by vocabulary now.
