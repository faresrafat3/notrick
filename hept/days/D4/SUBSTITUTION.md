## The machine in bland clothes
"An entry has: the check (with a way it could fail), the paste (raw output), the fee (paid before checking), the hold (terminal until its gap closes, inherited by copies), the tries (how often it was applied). Entries cite ancestors; nothing is edited after judgment; a mechanism with 3 tries and no stops is retired."

## Spine check — does the generating move survive?
The generating move (D3 niche): **refuse every success claim that cannot re-run its own test in front of you.**
- Under the rename: "don't accept a claim you can't re-check" — the refusal, the re-check requirement, and the externality (in front of you = paste from a real run) all survive intact.
- The fails-when set (X1-X8) survives: every forbidden move is expressible in bland words (self-chosen check, retyped value, after-the-fact approval, unpriced check...).
- The teeth-tests (I1-I5) survive: byte-compare, timestamp diff, edge walk, hash compare, (tries, stops) pair — none needs a distinctive term.

## Verdict
SPINE SURVIVES — the machine is not branding. The distinctive vocabulary (RECORD, MARK, TRACE...) is ergonomic, not load-bearing. Conversely this means: **the doctrine's content is procedural, not terminological** — consistent with the COULD_HAVE downgrade (novelty = the five fields, not any naming scheme).
