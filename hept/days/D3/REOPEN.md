# REOPEN — D3-Q08

4 of 8 KEEP claims reopened and attacked. Friendly = FAIL, so each attack is real, not ceremonial.

## BK1 reopened: "no re-runnable criterion → not a success"
**Kill attempt:** the criterion itself is written by a fallible writer; a frozen-but-wrong criterion produces confident wrong verdicts (a criterion checking the wrong invariant passes garbage forever). If frozen criteria can be wrong, re-runnability guarantees reproducibility, not correctness — the claim overstates what re-running buys.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES, weakened. Re-running buys testability, not truth; BK1 must be read as "no criterion → no claim", never as "criterion → truth". Amended wording recorded; kept because the alternative (case-by-case human judgment) is strictly weaker here (V-001 class: the human IS the failure).

## BK3 reopened: "frozen BEFORE the attempt"
**Kill attempt:** pre-freezing is gameable at the meta level — write a criterion so broad it passes anything ("output exists"), freeze it, then run. The timing law (A1) does not require the criterion to be TIGHT, only EARLY. A broad early criterion defeats the whole mechanism while complying with it.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES with a new tooth. Freezing must include a rejection condition: a criterion that could not fail any conceivable output is not a criterion. New constraint enters Day 4: every criterion must name at least one output that would FAIL it. Without this patch BK3 was killable; it survives only as amended.

## BK5 reopened: "UNVERIFIED is terminal, not shameful"
**Kill attempt:** a terminal UNVERIFIED state can be laundered — restate the same claim with a trivial criterion added after the fact, get it verified, and the history of the original unverifiable claim disappears. The terminal flag doesn't prevent resurrection-by-rewriting.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES with hatch closed. Resurrection requires the new claim to cite the old one as its ancestor; the UNVERIFIED flag propagates to descendants (an UNVERIFIED ancestor makes the descendant UNVERIFIED until the specific gap is closed). Kept, amended.

## BK7 reopened: "zero catches → demote"
**Kill attempt:** the demotion rule kills mechanisms too young to have caught anything — a brand-new mechanism has zero catches on day one and would be executed by its own birth. The rule as stated is age-blind and would murder every seed mechanism, including the ones that later caught real violations.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES with scope fixed. Demotion requires a MEASURED OPPORTUNITY: N chances to catch (N = exposures, not days). A mechanism never exposed to a claim cannot be judged; a mechanism exposed ≥3 times with zero catches is demoted. Kept, amended — the original wording was genuinely broken and would have killed MIC-11's decoy traps.
