# RIVALS (D5-Q06)

Three rivals that explain the same corpus material. One is uncomfortably good.

## S1 — "Trust decay" (economic story)
The corpus is what happens when a low-trust environment meets a high-ambition document culture: because a previous agent fabricated (V-001/V-002), every subsequent artifact adds a defensive layer (pins, gates, charters, counters). The material is explained WITHOUT any verification doctrine: it is simply escalating distrust made visible in files. Predicts: layers keep accreting; nothing ever runs (matches: 40 files, 0 runs, 2 fabrications). uncomfortably good at explaining the ACCRETION pattern.
**Uncomfortably good? YES** — it explains why the corpus grew exactly the way it did (defense against the last failure), which the refusal doctrine does not attempt to explain.

## S2 — "One-writer theater" (structural story)
There is exactly one real writer per session (a single agent + one human). Every role separation (charters, council seats, MICs, supervisor chip) is one entity wearing multiple hats. The material is explained as solo theater: the verification machinery is a monologue with hats. Predicts: any gate is self-owned (F12 confirmed), ratification is retro-logged (DS8 confirmed), no live multi-agent run (D0005 confirmed). Explains the practiced object almost perfectly.
**Uncomfortably good? PARTIAL** — explains practice's shape, but cannot explain why the machinery CAUGHT its wearer twice (V-001, V-002) and once in rehearsal (DS-P-001). A pure theater has no reason to fail its own actor.

## S3 — "Prompt overfitting" (training story)
The corpus is a language model overfitting to its own boot documents: each file cites the previous files, growing a self-referential canon (CONTEXT.md as compaction; rcvm.md's self-specification Φ). Verification vocabulary is just the genre. Predicts: high internal citation density, low external contact (matches: citations are single-hop, R10 unexercised).
**Uncomfortably good? NO** — explains style, not the specific mechanisms (would-fail-if, counted exposure) that the corpus generated against its own failures.

## Elimination targets (Q07)
S1 and S3 explain accretion/style but none explains the CATCHES — the corpus's only non-trivial events. S2 explains everything EXCEPT the catches. Elimination in Q07 must handle the catch-problem honestly.

# E-ELIMINATION (D5-Q07)

## E1: S3 (prompt overfitting) — ELIMINATED
Kill test: S3 predicts style-without-mechanism. But the corpus contains mechanisms whose form is forced by specific failures, not by genre: the would-fail-if header (BK3 amendment exists ONLY because the broad-criterion hole was found by attacking it), and the exposure-counted demotion (BK7 exists ONLY because its original wording nearly murdered seeds). Genre does not defend itself against its own kill attempts; mechanisms do. Additionally S3 predicts LOW contact with failures — yet the corpus's three catches (V-001, V-002, DS-P-001) are precisely at its own failure points, not at genre-typical points. S3 cannot say why the failure points cluster there. Eliminated.

## E2: S1 (trust decay) — ELIMINATED
Kill test: S1 predicts ACCRETION without catches — defensive layers that pile up but never fire (distrust breeds ritual, not detection). The corpus's layers FIRED three times. Worse for S1: the doctrine DEMOLISHED layers during this week (D3 killed 5 destinies, banned 12 slogans, demoted dead weight, COULD_HAVE downgraded 40 files to five fields) — trust decay predicts only accretion, never demolition. The week's net effect is a SMALLER theory, which S1 cannot generate. Eliminated.

## Survivor: S2 (one-writer theater) — LIVE FORK
S2 survives because it explains the practiced object (self-owned gates, retro-logs, no runs) and cannot be dismissed: its one weakness (the catches) is explainable inside S2 as "the actor tripping over its own props" — plausible. The doctrine's answer to S2 is not rhetorical: it is the same answer the D3 verdict gave R2 — the machine must be executable by MORE than one writer to prove it is not theater. That test (multi-writer run) is exactly what the corpus has never done (D0005), and the doctrine says so rather than claiming otherwise.
**Live fork standing:** S2 remains live until a real multi-writer run exists (same re-entry condition family as R2's fork). No blend: the doctrine does not absorb S2's explanation; it names the experiment that would decide.
