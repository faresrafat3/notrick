## Interaction rule (anti-blend)
- The forks are independent: F-S2 can fire while F-R2 stays dormant, and vice versa. Neither may be resolved by absorbing the other.
- If BOTH fire: the doctrine does not merge them — it spawns a third-generation verdict (Day-7 Q14 territory), and the canon states the double defeat explicitly.
- If neither fires by week end: both remain listed in CANON's live-fork section as undecided, with their experiments attached — that is an honest output, not a failure.
