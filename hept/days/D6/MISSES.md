# MISSES (D6-Q11)

Four observations that would kill the theory if found. Each names the kill mechanism — not "it would be weaker".

## MISS-1 — A rule-compliant fabrication
**Observation that kills:** a record satisfying all seven rules (criterion, paste with timestamp+determinism class, price, marks, environment class, counted mechanism) whose claim is nevertheless false.
**Why it kills:** the theory's whole object is "refuse success claims that cannot re-run their own test"; a re-runnable, fully-traced false claim shows re-running is insufficient for truth — the refusal object collapses into "hygiene that doesn't reach validity" (the bug-tracker reduction would then cover EVERYTHING, GY15 un-exiled).
**Kill mechanism:** NC1 fires; MVT Rule 2's honesty-floor claim dies; canon must demote to "reliability of records" and drop the refusal-object sentence.

## MISS-2 — Inheritance strangles a healthy ledger
**Observation that kills:** a corpus where UNVERIFIED ancestors accumulate faster than gaps close, and >50% of live records are marked — with no fabrication present — purely through BK5 propagation.
**Why it kills:** Rule 4 would be a denial-of-service on the ledger: the mark system would be destroyed by its own inheritance (over-inheritance, NC2's sharpened defeat). The theory would have proven that honesty-marking cannot scale without a discretion layer it explicitly banned.
**Kill mechanism:** NC2 fires; Rule 4 needs per-gap scoping or inheritance limits — but since adding discretion is banned (X-family), the theory would have to accept either a banned repair or collapse of the ledger's usability.

## MISS-3 — Retirement converges on the load-bearing mechanisms
**Observation that kills:** the first ≥5 real retirements under Rule 6 include a mechanism whose catch class reappears through other routes at rate >0 (NC3's defeat-observation), i.e., retirement repeatedly executes the doctrine's own catchers.
**Why it kills:** Rule 6 would be an auto-immune disease — the counted-retirement machinery killing exactly the mechanisms doing the work; every later fabricator class would then go undetected by design.
**Kill mechanism:** NC3 fires; Rule 6 must be suspended or inverted; the counted-governance claim dies and GRAVEYARD's anti-resurrection rule becomes a liability (it would enforce the bad retirements).

## MISS-4 — The stall dominates a clean ledger
**Observation that kills:** a full corpus where every record passes every rule, yet the workspace's net claims are false because judgment accumulated in the excluded zone (queues, stalls, cascades) — the EX2 blind spot eating the building while the bricks were perfect (NC4's defeat-observation).
**Why it kills:** the scope cut itself (MUST_NOT 8) would be the fatal omission: the theory's refusal-scope would be demonstrably the wrong scope — checking per-record honesty while systemic dishonesty (deferred judgment) banks the real fates.
**Kill mechanism:** NC4 fires; the scope cut must be reverted, which requires absorbing governance (F-R2 fires with it); the MVT gains a verdict-rule or admits it never was a theory of the workspace, only of its records.

## Note
All four are live: MISS-1 is B1/NC1's already-named candidate (command forgery is its nearest cousin); MISS-2/3/4 have zero test instances so far (no large ledger, no retirement, no stall-dominance case). None may be declared "handled" — they are the theory's open kill-conditions, carried into CANON section 14.
