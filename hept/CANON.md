# CANON

## 1. Doctrine (≤8 lines)
A claim is acceptable only if: (1) its test was written before it ran and names an output property that would fail it; (2) its recorded values are verbatim pastes of real command output, timestamped by the command, inside a declared determinism class; (3) the check was paid for before it ran; (4) a failed-or-unrunnable test marks the claim — the mark names its exact gap and inherits to everything built on the claim; (5) records are never rewritten, only superseded by records that cite their ancestors; (6) mechanisms live or die only by counted tries-and-catches inside a pre-declared exposure class; (7) a pass names the environment class it ran in, and a verifier may re-run elsewhere — cross-class mismatch is fabrication. Records are the only witnesses. Nothing that cannot be shown in a record happened.

## 2. Niche / object as a cut
Refuse every success claim that cannot re-run its own test in front of you.
This object was chosen over governance (attribution/queue — suspended fork) and over bureaucracy and formal-verification readings, each of which was killed for paying fewer real debts. The object loses things by design: it does not model scheduling, does not judge agents, does not prevent fabrication — it detects and marks.

## 3. Primitives + illegal moves
Primitives (bland names final): RECORD (judged append-only unit), CRITERION (frozen test + fail condition + class), TRACE (timestamped paste + determinism class), MARK (exact gap-set, terminal, inheriting), PRICE (committed cost pair), CLASS (pre-declared exposure class).
Illegal moves: X1 self-chosen criterion after seeing the attempt; X2 retyped values; X3 retro-legalized approval; X4 queue-as-result; X5 editing judged records; X6 pass without a fail condition; X7 unpriced checks; X8 taste-based demotion; X9 unreachable fail conditions; X10 undeclared exposure denominators; X11 stale criteria passing claims; X12 author-marking their own record.

## 4. Engine
Generators: audit-one (re-run a trace), criterion-critic (fail condition + reachability), chain-walk (laundering), catch-banker (retire on counted zero), price-audit (pre-commit), ancestor-hole (citation demand), fork-trigger (fires only when the paste-rule is insufficient), stall-accountant (invoices, never verdicts). Filters: the illegal moves + the seven doctrine clauses. Scoring: depth, surprise-with-necessity, debt-payment, coherence — sounding-smart scores zero. Execution precedent: a 10-call chain on real files with 9 recordable products and 1 recorded dead-end.

## 5. Because-spine (surviving KEEP reasons)
- Frozen criteria WITH fail conditions (instead of broad tests): BECAUSE a test that cannot fail is not a test; INSTEAD-OF process-compliance-as-quality; IF-FALSE a broad criterion still catches real fabrications, the rule is ceremony.
- Paste-only values (instead of typed plausible values): BECAUSE both recorded fabrications were typed values; INSTEAD-OF trust-the-agent; IF-FALSE a fabricated paste passes all checks (live hole, Rule 7 bounds it).
- Terminal inheriting marks (instead of delete-or-fund): BECAUSE deleting hides the diagnosis loop and funding lies; INSTEAD-OF bug-tracker advisory blocks; IF-FALSE over-inheritance strangles a healthy ledger (live kill-condition).
- Counted retirement (instead of taste): BECAUSE elegance murdered a seed rule in its own wording test; INSTEAD-OF senior-review judgment; IF-FALSE retirement repeatedly kills working mechanisms (live kill-condition).
- Append-only supersession (instead of edits): BECAUSE the silent header overwrite was fabrication class; INSTEAD-OF version-control pragmatism; IF-FALSE the ledger becomes unwritable under correction load.
- Environment declaration (instead of trusted shell): BECAUSE the command is the attacker's surface; INSTEAD-OF sandbox faith; IF-FALSE cross-class mismatches turn out to be noise at scale.
- Refusal scope (instead of governance): BECAUSE the governance rival pays fewer debts without a run; INSTEAD-OF queue-verdict theories; IF-FALSE a fully-compliant corpus with a false net claim (stall-dominance) — recorded as untested, not settled.
