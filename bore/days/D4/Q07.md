# D4-Q07 — generator: 6 steps that emit a legal next move of the SAME kind

Not a global engine. The generator is local to this fragment's kind: priced bounds on a draw-check scenario, in the hole's own grammar (backtick formulas, dash separators, honesty label, negative boundary for small k). Input: a schema state (N, k, s, t, trigger) from D4-Q04; the hole's own formulas from D4-Q05.

## The 6 steps

- G1 | TAKE a schema state (N, k, s, t, trigger) — all fields from F1-F5.
- G2 | CHOOSE one operation the hole actually performs: O2 price (P(catch), P(evade)) or O4 control (next-s on trigger). Nothing else (O1/O3/O5 are not sentence-emitters; O5 is ordering, not content).
- G3 | COMPUTE with the hole's own formulas only: k/N for s=1; 1 − C(N−k,s)/C(N,s) without replacement; (1−P(catch))^t for evasion across t tasks. No foreign probability.
- G4 | STATE the number as a bound with the hole's honesty label: "near-useless alone" when the evasion > ~0.9, else "over t tasks ≈ X"; keep "Stated plainly so nobody oversells it" harvestable.
- G5 | APPEND the boundary exactly when the number is honest-weak: if k is small relative to N, the sentence carries the hole's negative (sampling alone does not deter small fraud); if k large, the positive (wholesale is deterred). No middle case exists in the hole's vocabulary.
- G6 | EMIT in the hole's grammar: backtick math, "≈", em-dash repairs, ≤1 clause of hedge ("(candidate)" style) only when the claim is a control rule, never when it is a price.

## Legal/illegal moves of the generator

- LEGAL: “`N=500, k=2, s=1, t=5`: `P(evade) = (1-2/500)^5 ≈ 0.98` — near-useless alone. Stated plainly so nobody oversells it: small `k` still walks.” (G1-G6 applied: state, O2, k/N, 0.98 label, k-small boundary, hole grammar.)
- LEGAL control-emission: “Escalation note (candidate): on `MISMATCH` next task is `s=full`; effect unpriced here.” (G4's hedge clause carried because it is control, not price.)
- ILLEGAL: any emission that names hash, honor-cost, combination, or danger-signal operating as strength — those have no schema slot (D4-Q04) and no operation (D4-Q03).
- ILLEGAL: any emission computing an expected VALUE over the schema (e.g. “expected fakes caught = s·k/N·t”) — the hole never performs expectation, only priced bounds and evasion.

## Why 6 steps and not fewer

G4 and G5 are the hole's DNA: a price sentence without the honesty label is not the same KIND (it becomes a neutral formula), and without the k-size boundary it loses the only unhedged content (N2). G6 encodes the hole's own em-dash/backtick/repair grammar so an emitted move is indistinguishable in kind. The generator emits ONE kind of sentence (priced bound on the draw-check), not theories.
