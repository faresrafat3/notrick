# D4-Q06 — EX2: the sentence that does not fit — honest snag

Source: bore/HOLE.md only. EX1's machine = (N,k,s,t,trigger) → price + next-s (D4-Q05). Which sentence refuses to run on it?

## The snag

"Strength comes from the组合: cheap hash (binds the whole output against post-hoc edits) + danger escalation + repetition + honor-cost visibility." (L6)

## Why it does not fit

1. Nothing in the sentence is a function of the schema. The machine's four inputs are N, k, s, t + trigger; the sentence names hash, escalation, repetition, honor-cost. Of those, only repetition = t (F4) has a slot, and the sentence uses it as a COUNTER-weight against the other three, none of which are machine fields. So four of the five words have no slot (D4-Q04: "no slot for rescue") — the sentence describes a different machine than the one the fragment prices.
2. Its grammatical role is adversative-rescue: "Strength comes from the组合" is positioned as the answer to "sampling alone only deters ... never surgical" (L6-first-half). But the machine's own pricing CONTRADICTS the rescue's hierarchy: escalation (s=1→s=3) is the machine's weakest lever (0.05→0.14 per task, D3-Q08/EXLAW), yet the sentence names it second among four strengths — a strength list whose first named lever is the machine's most inert one.
3. The sentence asserts a WHOLE-output claim ("binds the whole output") against a machine whose pricing is per-line (O2: k/N). The fragment's arithmetic is line-level; the sentence's rescue claim is output-level. Two different units of analysis in one 7-line text.
4. It is the only sentence in the hole that cannot be evaluated by the machine: plug any (N,k,s,t,trigger) and you get a price or a next-s; plug it into L6 and you get nothing the machine computes. It is dead weight by the fragment's own operating standard (O1-O5 are the operations; L6 operates none).

## Why this snag is honest (not a strawman)

The sentence is not an error of the fragment — it is exactly the promised rescue that D2-Q07 showed unpaid and D3-Q10 showed costume. EX2's contribution: it isolates WHERE the break is, in machine terms: the fragment jumps from a line-level pricing machine to an output-level rescue sentence, and nothing in the machine's vocabulary can carry that jump. That is the fragment's structural seam, not just its rhetoric.
