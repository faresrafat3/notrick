## Skeleton

```
SCHEMA: (N, k, s, t, trigger)  — lines N, k faked, draw s now, t tasks in, last signal trigger
PRICING:
  P(catch)   = k/N                      (single draw, any s where s>=1 hits k-lines uniformly)
  P(catch,s) = 1 - C(N-k,s)/C(N,s)      (s draws without replacement)
  P(evade,t) = (1 - P(catch))^t         (independent tasks)
CONTROL:
  next-s: trigger=none -> 1 ; trigger=danger -> 3 ; trigger=MISMATCH -> full
  full = re-check every line (unpriced by formula; level, not integer)
BOUND (the only unhedged line):
  small k is not deterred: (N=100,k=1,s=1,t=5) -> P(evade) ≈ 0.951
  large k and lazy lies are deterred; surgical single-line forgery is NOT
```

## What the skeleton exposes

- The machine has FOUR lines: schema, pricing, control, bound. That is the whole load-bearing fragment (D3-Q09: N1-N3 + the arithmetic).
- The skeleton CAN be run: pick (N,k,s,t,trigger), get a price, get a next-s. That is the Day-4 done-when target — a stranger can produce one more legal move.
- Everything D3-Q10 called costume (the rescue sentence, escalation-as-recovery, hash binding, honor-cost visibility, danger list, Owner hedge) is absent, because none of it operates the machine (D4-Q03: unexecuted names).
