# D2-Q05 — silence inside the hole: 6 questions the fragment should have to face and does not

Source: bore/HOLE.md only. Each question is one the fragment's own wording raises and leaves unaddressed within lines 12-18.

- S1 | What does the checker do when the draw returns a line that is NOT among the `k` faked? The hole only prices the hit case (P(catch)) and the escape (P(evade)); it never states what a clean draw proves about the OTHER N−s lines. Silence on what a miss means is the fragment's central omission — the price is computed, the product (what one clean line buys about the rest) is not.
- S2 | Who chooses the sampler index AFTER the claimant commits, and how is the order enforced in paper Phase A? L3 says "verifier samples s=1" but never says how the verifier is barred from choosing the one honest line, nor how the claimant is barred from editing after the reveal. §1 (outside the hole) names commit-then-reveal; inside the hole the guarantee is absent.
- S3 | On `danger-signal s=3`: what if the danger is itself fabricated by the claimant (the budget-echo mismatch is invented) or by the verifier (the mismatch is claimed to force s=3)? The hole treats danger as a reliable trigger; it never considers danger-deception.
- S4 | `every 3rd claim` escalates — by what calendar? If claims arrive 10 per day, s=3 hits constantly and honoring-cost stops being near-zero; if 1 per day, escalation barely ever fires. The hole never ties escalation rate to claim volume, so "every 3rd" is volume-blind.
- S5 | What is the cost of `s=full`? The schedule escalates to full verification as the ultimate threat, but the fragment never states that full re-run cost — the very quantity §3 (outside) calls `C_fullverify`. Inside the hole, the nuclear option has no price tag, so the escalation threat is unpriced.
- S6 | What does "honor-cost visibility" make visible, and to whom? L4 lists it as strength; L7 never names what the ledger shows (minutes? who sees it? who audits it?). Visibility is asserted as deterrent (A7 in §4, outside) but inside the hole it is a name without an object.

All 6 questions are answerable only outside lines 12-18 — which is exactly why they are the fragment's silence, not the file's.
