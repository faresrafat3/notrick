# D5-Q03 — formalism-is-costume test on the Day-4 machine

Test: strip the D4 machine's formal presentation (backticks, symbols, formulas) and rewrite it in plain prose. If the substance dies without the formalism, the machine was costume; if it survives, the formalism was packaging.

## The machine in plain prose (no backticks, no symbols)

- A batch has N items, k of them bad. The checker looks at s items.
- A single random look finds a bad item with probability k/N.
- A miss escapes across t rounds with probability (1 − k/N) to the t.
- With s looks and no replacement, the miss is (1 − C(N−k,s)/C(N,s)).
- If the look misses, every bad item is provably in the part not looked at.
- On an alert, look at 3; on a mismatch, look at everything next round.
- Small k escapes; the scheme's only honest statement is that a cheap look cannot catch a one-bad-item batch.

## Verdict

SURVIVES. The plain-prose machine says everything the formal one did: the negative-clearance theorem (miss localizes bad items to the blind spot), the escalation ladder, the s=full punishment, the small-k boundary. Nothing that the fragment's findings depend on (D4-T1 theorem, D4-T2 cost, the bound) needed the symbols. The formalism (backticked formulas, C(95,3)/C(100,3) notation, the ≈ glyphs) added precision of expression, zero substance.

BUT — one nuance keeps the formalism from being pure costume: the FORMULA is what makes the finding VERIFIABLE. "Miss localizes bad items to the blind spot" is a claim; k/N and C(N−k,s)/C(N,s) are the mechanism that lets a reader recompute it (D4-Q08 ran real numbers). So the formalism's job here is evidence-legibility, not theory. Distinction: costume = formalism that implies a theory the prose lacks; evidence-form = formalism that lets the prose be checked. This machine's formalism is evidence-form, and its THEORY is still the thin textbook arithmetic D5-Q01 reduced.
