## §0 — Thesis

The tower named the system's **static anatomy**: seven layers, one entrance each, every
index generated from disk (R14). What it left open is the **dynamic half**. From the
driver's seat, NOTRICK is not a stack of files — it is one **operating cycle** that every
agent, charter, and lane runs, at every scale (§2): BOOT → ORIENT → CLAIM → ADJUDICATE →
EXECUTE-PAPER → VERIFY → RECORD → ACCRETE → WAIT → RETIRE. And every file in the tree
does exactly one of **three surface jobs** (§3): **GENERATE** (produce truth from disk at
read time — R14), **ROUTE** (point to where truth lives — never restate it), **RECORD**
(append what happened — never rewrite it). The remaining measured debts are leaks where a
file does two jobs, or a phase of the cycle has no cheap entrance: the boot chain's first
file is its heaviest and its hand-written head lies by staleness (M8); more than half the
tree has no semantic map (M9); citation rot was the most-documented pain and stayed
unmechanized until today (M10, now `tools/citecheck.sh`); the zero-day plan drifts from
the state it plans for (M11); and the lane conventions the board itself mandates are
followed by zero lanes (M12). Four one-word Owner parts close them (pr-024 A–D).

The cycle is fractal by the arc law (system · charter · task · hypothesis · attempt ·
audit) — the tower's layers are its anatomy, this table is its physiology. An ergonomic
defect is always one of two things: **a phase with no cheap entrance**, or **a file doing
two surface jobs**. That is the whole diagnostic.

## §3 — The three-surface invariant (GENERATE / ROUTE / RECORD)

- **GENERATE** — the truth comes from disk at read time (audit.sh · citecheck · the
  INTEGRITY verify command). Protected by R14 + live consumption.
- **ROUTE** — points, one line each, never restated content (README · ONBOARDING role
  paths · OWNER-DESK · LANES · BRIEF). Its disease is **duplication** — a route that
  restates becomes a second truth and rots on a timer.
- **RECORD** — append-only history (CONTEXT tail · LOG · receipts · violations ·
  breaker logs). Its disease is **rewriting** — and, when it also carries a state
  summary, the summary rots while the record stays legally intact.

**The accretion test:** before landing any new artifact, an agent declares its surface
type. A file that GENERATEs and ROUTEs (proposals/README before pr-023-F — "no proposals
yet" vs 32 files) or RECORDs and GENERATEs (CONTEXT's hand-written head — "38 provider
keys" against BRIEF:17's 57) is a lie on a timer. The invariant explains every disease
the tower found and every one found today; it is the one-paragraph law of "what may a
new file be".
