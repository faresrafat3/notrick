# CLAIMS-V1 — تفكيك النظرية لادعاءات (سيشن الهدم)
> المصدر: FOUNDATIONAL-BRIEF.md "Doctrine in one line" + CONTEXT.md "The project (in one sentence)". الوثائق على القرص هي الهدف المهدوم؛ هذا الملف تجسيد النظرية.

## النظرية في 5 جمل

1. **التنسيق بلا منسّق:** عقل يُربّى من الأسفل — وحدات دقيقة (MICs) + أربع وكالات ميثاقية — يتنسّق حصرًا عبر مستندات مُثبّتة ونتائج موثّقة على القرص، بلا منسّق حي (stigmergy: البيئة القابلة للكتابة هي الوسيط الوحيد).
2. **المستندات الصوت الوحيد:** لا كيان يتكلم بحضوره أو نيّته، بل بكتابته الموثّقة — ما لا يُرى في ملف لم يحدث (AGENTS.md: "If it cannot be shown in a document, it did not happen").
3. **النتائج الأسعار الوحيدة:** مصير كل شيء (توسيع/تهذيب/تقاعد) يُحسم من سجلات نواتج موثّقة لا من الآراء ولا من كاريزما صاحبها.
4. **البوابة الحقيقة الوحيدة:** كل ادعاء نجاح يمرّ ببوابة لا يملكها المُدّعي — إعادة تشغيل من قياس خارجي منفصل، ونطق المخرج حرفيًا لا تلخيصه.
5. **المالك المصادِق الوحيد:** القانون والعتبة وتعريف الاختراق والـGO حقول تُملأ بيده وحده، وكل ما عداه يبقى فارغًا بحقه — تصديق الغير باطل مهما مُوّه.

## ب-1 القوانين R — اقتباس سطر واحد لكل قانون (CL-01..11)

- **CL-01 · R1** (RULES.md:6): "Live messaging that repeats the SAME pattern ≥3 times across ≥2 sessions MUST be frozen into a document format. A document format repeated ≥3 times MAY move into a live protocol. Transaction costs decide — not taste, not elegance."
- **CL-02 · R2** (RULES.md:11): "Charters are versioned. When a charter grows past one page → re-forge compact as v+1 (append-only refinement log moves to an annex). Fixed form, moving content."
- **CL-03 · R3** (RULES.md:15): "A wall failed 3× on one axis is not a shame record — it is an escalation trigger AND an asset: the axis ledger feeds the Prime's next angle. Budget exhaustion = stop + hand back + report. A run that ends on budget fed the ledger; persisting past budget is the only unforgivable spend."
- **CL-04 · R5** (RULES.md:21): "Every result record carries a K-line (active charters · axis · wall · gate outcome). A result without a K-line is not indexable — the Curator refuses it."
- **CL-05 · R6** (RULES.md:25): "Any claimed success passes a gate it does not own. A gate owned by the claimer is not a gate."
- **CL-06 · R7** (RULES.md:29): "A new charter is forged ONLY on: a documented NO NICHE call, or an explicit Owner demand. Never on enthusiasm, never 'for completeness'."
- **CL-07 · R8** (RULES.md:36): "The Owner is the terminus of every appeal chain: council verdicts, verifier gates, charter ratifications — overturned only by the Owner IN WRITING, never silently."
- **CL-08 · R9** (RULES.md:40): "Action is public: it requires at least ONE other seat to witness or object. A judgment made in complete isolation (no council seat, no Verifier gate, no Owner) is not a system act — it is a private opinion and cannot be cited."
- **CL-09 · R10** (RULES.md:46): "No foundation-and-build, no coherence-circle: evidence is a CROSSWORD. Every new claim must intersect already-placed entries (documented results, K-lines, existing charters) at every crossing point. A claim that intersects nothing (no citations, no gates, no charters) does not enter the grid. A claim that contradicts a settled entry is rejected even if beautiful."
- **CL-10 · R11** (RULES.md:54): "Design-time doctrine (charters, rulings, research, templates) lives under `~/Projects/` — a system under DESIGN is not a system under OPERATION. Runtime containers (~/.openclaw/, ~/.dsh/) hold only executable state. Philosophy belongs to the workshop, not the machine room."
- **CL-11 · R12** (RULES.md:60): "Anything inside an agent's workspace is writable by that agent by design. Never store irreplaceable doctrine inside an agent's own workspace — doctrine goes to Projects; the agent workspace keeps only ephemeral task artifacts."
