## The principle — the pre-registered obituary
**Nothing exists in NOTRICK without its death written first.** A charter is born with its
retirement trigger already signed; a task with its abort criteria; a hypothesis with its
verdict rule; a proposal with its expiry. This is "the start of the end at the end of the
start": birth only COMPLETES when the obituary is drafted — because an entity that cannot
die cannot be honest (it will optimize existing over delivering — the Factorio pattern at
the population level), and an entity whose death is unwritten will be killed by politics
instead of by evidence.

Honest novelty check: pre-registration exists (clinical trials), definition-of-done exists
(agile), post-mortems exist (SRE). The novel composition: **death as a first-class citizen
AT BIRTH, for every entity class in an agent system, enforced by one uniform loop.**

## ALPHA-7 — the Gate of Birth (seven questions; all seven or no birth)
Anything entering NOTRICK — charter, task, hypothesis, rule, proposal, MIC, audit — answers:
1. **HOLE (Gause):** which exact unfilled burrow does it fill? cite the collision check.
2. **CLAIM (R10):** what does it assert, crossing which already-placed entries?
3. **BUDGET (R3):** what does it cost, and what stops it?
4. **OBITUARY (Omega-draft):** under which OBSERVED condition does it die? (pre-registered,
   evidence-detectable — "when it stops being useful" is not an obituary, it is a shrug)
5. **MEASURER (disinterest):** who verifies — and why are they not the beneficiary?
6. **SLOT (R5):** where in the K-line constellation does its memory live?
7. **HEIR (continuity):** if it dies mid-arc, what inherits its lesson?

## OMEGA-5 — the Gate of Death (five duties at ANY ending)
At completion, retirement, dissolution, failure, or Owner cancellation — identical duties:
1. **VERDICT:** the fate word (complete/retire/dissolve/abort) — cited from the obituary
   clause that FIRED (no narrative exits; the pre-written condition is quoted).
2. **LESSON:** one line extracted for the Curator (the only thing that survives for sure).
3. **BURIAL:** the K-line closes — memory is the constellation of FINISHED arcs.
4. **HEIR:** what continues — an heir named, or "orphan" declared and the Owner notified.
5. **LEDGER:** the ending logged. **No silent exits.** An entity that vanishes without
   Omega-duties is not retired — it is LOST, and the loss is a violation.
