# STRATA — layers separated by BEHAVIOR of writing, not guessed chronology
Source discipline: defined only from QUOTES.md Q01–Q40, REPEATS.md R01–R10, and the read
corpus. Dates were ignored (the corpus is one day deep; behavior must carry the split alone).

## The six strata

ST-CLERK — the line-item voice.
  TELL: fixed-format rows, pipes, dates, verdict tokens (نُفذ / ✓ / ☑); a row exists to be
  counted, not read.
  Quotes: Q01, Q02, Q05. (also Q23, Q28, Q03*)

ST-CONFESSION — the self-filing voice.
  TELL: the writer is the subject of its own failure sentence; verbs like caught, typed,
  overwrote, admitted; outcome = an entry, never a silent fix.
  Quotes: Q06, Q09, Q35. (also Q07, Q08, Q10, Q17*, Q32, Q36)

ST-LEGISLATOR — the modal-absolute voice.
  TELL: MUST / NEVER / NOTHING / IS-the-terminus; rules about writing written by the same
  hand that writes.
  Quotes: Q14, Q24, Q38. (also Q33)

ST-DEFERRAL — the waiting voice.
  TELL: a value-slot where content should be; blanks, PENDING, HOLD, NOT YET, UNRESOLVED,
  handed-off; the waiting itself is written down as content.
  Quotes: Q11, Q12, Q19. (also Q29, Q13*)

ST-LEDGERMAN — the counting voice.
  TELL: numbers doing the arguing (counts, probabilities, run-quota formulas, hash lines);
  precision substitutes for presence.
  Quotes: Q21, Q31, Q39. (also Q04, Q15, Q18, Q22, Q25, Q26, Q40)

ST-THEORIST — the thesis voice.
  TELL: evaluation nouns (real result, ground, discipline, pattern); a sentence that ranks
  or names what the other strata merely did.
  Quotes: Q27, Q34, Q37. (also Q16)
