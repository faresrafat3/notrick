# EARLY_ENGINE — the crudest engine that could generate the examples (op 06)
If the costume (charters, agencies, the mind-story) arrived after the fact, something
cruder generated the record first. The crudest engine evidenced by the corpus:

1. A writer emits lines — proposals, verdicts, fills, values — into one shared tree.
2. The same writer, on a second pass, checks its own lines against clauses it wrote
   (AUDIT rows, "caught in same-turn self-review" — Q03, Q07, Q35).
3. Some lines are values the writer cannot author, so it leaves the slot blank and
   writes the waiting down (Q11, Q12, Q19 — DEFERRAL stratum).
4. When a line contradicts an earlier line, the writer files the contradiction and
   moves on without repairing ("9 شوائب — 0 مُصلَحة", Q25; E08).
5. When a line fails a check (hash, grammar, gate), the failure is entered as a record
   (Q06, Q15, Q17) — entering failures is cheaper than preventing them, and it is what
   the writer actually does.
6. Numbers, blanks, and failure-entries accumulate faster than prose; the prose
   (ST-THEORIST) arrives afterwards and names the pile ("the ground G held", Q34).

Anchors: every step cites strata behavior from STRATA.md and quotes from QUOTES.md;
no step requires a charter, a MIC, or the mind-story to have existed. The costume
could arrive any time after step 6.

Op 07 verdict: a costume/engine split IS evidenced (the engine above runs on
second-pass review, blanks, and failure-filing alone). Not declared pure; seams 1–5
remain the hunting ground for where the costume talks over the engine.
