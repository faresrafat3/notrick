# DISCREPANCY LAW (verbatim from the kernel)

Legal kinds: D-USAGE D-EXAMPLE D-REPEAT D-AVOID D-REPAIR D-EXCESS D-VOICE D-PROMISE
Illegal: X-MOOD X-CRITIC X-SHADOW-BRAND X-HELP X-BLEND X-PRESTIGE

A discrepancy requires:
- WHERE: path + quote/fragment
- OFFICIAL MOVE: what the text pretends is happening
- PRACTICE MOVE: what is actually happening
- KIND
- COST: what the official object loses if practice is taken as law

If COST is "it would be weaker/more nuanced", FAIL.
