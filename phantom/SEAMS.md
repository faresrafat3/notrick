# SEAMS — five places two strata fight inside one paragraph (ops 03–04)

SEAM-1 · violations.md, V-001 entry. CLERK opens (ledger row: V-{NN} · date · agent ·
  violation · evidence) and mid-entry collapses into CONFESSION/THEORIST narrative
  ("Pattern note for the Owner: both incidents occurred during multi-write turns…").
  Official winner: the ledger — the format is law ("Nothing is silently fixed" needs rows).
  Practice winner: the narrative — the row records THAT, only the paragraph explains WHY;
  the take-away lives in the seam's losing side.

SEAM-2 · closeout §6, final paragraph. Five CONFESSION incident reports ("D1", "V-001",
  "The clobber"…) then ST-THEORIST arrives: "That is the session's real result: the ground
  G held — honesty by structure, not character."
  Official winner: THEORIST — the file ends on the thesis; the lesson is the point.
  Practice winner: the incidents — every load-bearing fact in the paragraph is theirs;
  the thesis sentence adds no evidence, it harvests theirs.

SEAM-3 · pr-006 §2. ST-LEDGERMAN computes ("P(evade) = (1-1/100)^5 ≈ 0.951") and
  ST-CONFESSION undercuts in the same breath ("near-useless alone. Stated plainly so
  nobody oversells it.").
  Official winner: LEDGERMAN — the proposal exists to sell the mechanism.
  Practice winner: CONFESSION — the printed number refutes the mechanism it advertises;
  the honesty token is the defeat.

SEAM-4 · RATIFICATION-BATCH-001 Part 5. CLERK skeleton ("Ruling: {AUTHORIZED | HOLD}")
  against the scribe's DEFERRAL/LEGISLATOR paragraph ("AUTHORIZED was NOT scribed: a new
  power grant with no prior Owner ruling to restate…").
  Official winner: the skeleton — the form is what governs ("the agent may add NOTHING").
  Practice winner: the paragraph — all substance (why HOLD, how to reverse) lives in the
  scribe's prose; the skeleton has become a mask the paragraph speaks through.

SEAM-5 · CONTEXT, VERSION STAMP REQUESTED entry. ST-LEGISLATOR ("does not parse under
  VERSION.md's pinned scale… stamping a malformed value … was refused") fights
  ST-DEFERRAL ("AWAITING OWNER PICK … three-option pick … remains Owner-only").
  Official winner: LEGISLATOR — the grammar refused the author; discipline preserved.
  Practice winner: DEFERRAL — the refusal produced no value, only a stall that was then
  written down and handed off twice (Q19); what moved: nothing.
