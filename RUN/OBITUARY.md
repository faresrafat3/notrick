# OBITUARY — THE RUN'S OWN OBITUARY (R0, written before the series does anything)

## The clause that would kill this series

> **If, by R14, the series has produced ZERO kills — nothing deleted, refuted, fenced,
> or demoted — then the series was theater and retires itself via Omega-5:
> verdict, lesson, burial, heir, ledger.**

## Why this clause
A verification series that keeps everything it touches has verified nothing: the only
respectable outcome of auditing a self-contradicting repo is that SOMETHING dies — a
stale count demoted, a decorative gate named, a frozen reading confirmed dead, a fake
claim fenced, an artifact retired. Zero kills means the audit was a costume (F0029,
F0030, PRESTIGE_INVERT).

## Measurer (may not be the author of the series)
The measurer of this obituary is the **R14 GHOST subagent** — a fresh context, no series
memory, paid to find what the series hid (R14 rounds 1-4: GHOST diff / AUDITOR citations /
FORENSIC find-one-thing / SKEPTIC proves-the-series-fake). It counts kills independently
of the series author and reports the number in R14's receipt. It was named here, at R0,
before the series did anything, so it cannot be appointed retroactively to rubber-stamp.

## Measurement rules
- A kill counts only if it is visible on disk: an Omega-5 ledger entry, a demotion row,
  a retracted/ move, a fence row that names a real catch, a proposal that retires a claim.
- A kill that R13's MEASURER would call cosmetic (killing something already dead) is
  subtracted by that same measurer (R13 brief).
- If the count is zero at R14: the series writes the verdict (theater), the lesson, the
  burial (RUN/ retires with a header naming its heir), the heir, the ledger entry — and
  R14 runs its audit, not its celebration.
