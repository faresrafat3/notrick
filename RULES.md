# RULES.md — living system rules (law-simple, thought-deep)

> Every rule = one law + where it lives + what breaks it. Append-only until a rule collides
> with another (then the Council arbitrates and both get re-forged).

## R1 — Interface evolution (Coase)
Live messaging that repeats the SAME pattern ≥3 times across ≥2 sessions MUST be frozen into
a document format. A document format repeated ≥3 times MAY move into a live protocol.
Transaction costs decide — not taste, not elegance.

## R2 — Charter maintenance (Sutton) — merged with R3's scroll rule
Charters are versioned. When a charter grows past one page → re-forge compact as v+1
(append-only refinement log moves to an annex). Fixed form, moving content.

## R3 — Antifragile walls & budgets (Taleb) [R4 merged into R3 on 2026-09-06]
A wall failed 3× on one axis is not a shame record — it is an escalation trigger AND an asset:
the axis ledger feeds the Prime's next angle. Budget exhaustion = stop + hand back + report.
A run that ends on budget fed the ledger; persisting past budget is the only unforgivable spend.

## R5 — K-line completeness (Minsky)
Every result record carries a K-line (active charters · axis · wall · gate outcome).
A result without a K-line is not indexable — the Curator refuses it.

## R6 — No trick (Minsky + Factorio)
Any claimed success passes a gate it does not own. A gate owned by the claimer is not a gate.

## R7 — Birth on demand (niche law; per RJ-001, progressive birth is strategy, not law)
A new charter is forged ONLY on: a documented NO NICHE call, or an explicit Owner demand.
Never on enthusiasm, never "for completeness". (RJ-001 demoted *progressive/planned* birth to
strategy — a charter is still forged on demand, but the *ordering/no-niche call* is the way
progress happens, not a scheduled expansion.)

## R8 — Owner ratification (the loop-breaker)
The Owner is the terminus of every appeal chain: council verdicts, verifier gates, charter
ratifications — overturned only by the Owner IN WRITING, never silently.

## R9 — Plurality floor (Arendt)
Action is public: it requires at least ONE other seat to witness or object. A judgment made
in complete isolation (no council seat, no Verifier gate, no Owner) is not a system act —
it is a private opinion and cannot be cited. Natality: any entity MAY propose a new charter
or a new rule — beginning is the right of every burrow, ratified as always by the Owner.

## R10 — Foundherent evidence (Haack)
No foundation-and-build, no coherence-circle: evidence is a CROSSWORD. Every new claim must
intersect already-placed entries (documented results, K-lines, existing charters) at every
crossing point. A claim that intersects nothing (no citations, no gates, no charters) does
not enter the grid. A claim that contradicts a settled entry is rejected even if beautiful.


## R11 — Philosophy builds in Projects (location law)
Design-time doctrine (charters, rulings, research, templates) lives under `~/Projects/` —
a system under DESIGN is not a system under OPERATION. Runtime containers (~/.openclaw/,
~/.dsh/) hold only executable state. Philosophy belongs to the workshop, not the machine room.
(Named after the 2026-09-06 misplacement of NOTRICK into an agent workspace.)

## R12 — The workspace is public to its agent (security law)
Anything inside an agent's workspace is writable by that agent by design. Never store
irreplaceable doctrine inside an agent's own workspace — doctrine goes to Projects; the agent
workspace keeps only ephemeral task artifacts.

## R14 — Generated truth (the count-law) [adopted 2026-09-11 via pr-023-B]
Any count, status, index, or tally presented to agents or the Owner on an authoritative
surface MUST be generated from the records at read time (a tool's verbatim output) or
live-computed — hand-maintained counts on such surfaces are banned, because a live tree
with a static count is a lie on a timer (born from: V-003, INTEGRITY "42"≠44;
proposals/README "no proposals yet" vs 32 files; the static-counts-rot lesson, 2026-09-06).
A broken generator is a broken gate: its failure is filed, never silently re-trusted.
A generated card is a witness of a moment, quotable per-run — never a law; where a card
and a record disagree, the record wins.
Falsifier: if a generated surface is caught lying to its reader before the records do,
this law dies into the obituary registry and the surface returns to hand-maintenance
with its rot on the record.

## R15 — Artifact language (English-only) [adopted 2026-09-11 via Owner directive]
All project artifacts — doctrine, indexes, living surfaces, tool output, and new entries
in LOG/CONTEXT — are written in English (best for agents: tokens, citations, grep-ability).
Chat with the Owner stays in Arabic for ease of communication. Historical records stay as
written (append-only; superseded, never rewritten): pre-2026-09-11 LOG/CONTEXT entries,
filed proposals, the terminal OPS_QUEUE, lane files, and verbatim Owner quotations.
Falsifier: if English-only measurably degrades verification (citations, audits) versus
the Arabic originals, this rule dies into the obituary registry and the workspace
re-bilingualizes by a new Owner line.
