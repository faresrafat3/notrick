## LEGAL reason

**BECAUSE:** Q0010 is the *operational form* of niche law (C0016 / LB06). It does not
derive from "niches are good"; it derives from Gause (exclusion) + Ricardo (comparative
advantage) + Goodhart containment (a metric scoped to a burrow cannot be gamed outside
it) + compartmentalization (context overflow is the LLM failure mode) — decision-review.md
D-01 L6-9 names all four. Each is independently testable; H1 is the conjunction
empirical claim those four jointly motivate. The reason names the *four* and the
*measurement*, not the conclusion.

**INSTEAD-OF:** A claim that "niches are good because specialization is generally
good." The rival is the obvious framing and matches most multi-agent literature. It
loses on a single thing: it has no falsifier — there is no observation that would
*re-forge* the doctrine (decision-review.md D-01 L10-11 is exactly that falsifier:
"≥3 recorded tasks, generalist wins on quality AND scope-completeness → re-forge via
Maker"). H1 supplies the falsifier; the rival does not. Without a falsifier, a claim
cannot be load-bearing (it cannot be killed).

**IF-FALSE:** If H1 is read as a posture ("we believe in niches") rather than an
empirical claim, then C0022/LB09's falsifier stops being a falsifier and becomes a
posture too. Niche law stops being falsifiable. R7 (RULES.md L28: "forged ONLY on a
documented NO NICHE call or an explicit Owner demand") loses its empirical anchor and
becomes ornamental. Maker (LB10) loses its retirement trigger (C0032: "when NO NICHE
calls stop coming and Owner demands stop"). Three downstream collapses named in the
records, not in prose.

**T-COST:** Accepting this reason forbids any defense of niche law that cannot name a
counter-task (or set of counter-tasks) on which a generalist beats it on BOTH quality
AND scope-completeness. The conjunction is the unit; "quality alone" or "scope alone"
is not the falsifier.
