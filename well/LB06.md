## FAKE reason (killed)

Killed because: "founding principle" / "built around it" is X-AUTHORITY + X-HOLISM; no
predicate is named. Fails T-NONCIRC ("built around it" re-uses "niche" with no new
content). Illegal kind: **X-AUTHORITY** + **X-HOLISM**.

## LEGAL reason

**BECAUSE:** RJ-001 L11 demoted Maker from axiom to *application* of the niche law.
The demotion is the predicate: niche law is now the *only* law from which birth derives;
Maker is a corollary. Before the demotion, two predicates competed for `BIRTH(ε)` —
axiom-Maker and niche-law; the demotion eliminates one. The claim is load-bearing
because it is now the *sole* generator of entity birth in the doctrine, with
decision-review.md D-01 L6-11 supplying the testable alternative the axiom-Maker did
not have.

**INSTEAD-OF:** A rival that "entities are born on documented demand" (which sounds
like the same rule). The rival is the literal phrasing of R7 ("RULES.md L28: forged
ONLY on a documented NO NICHE call or an explicit Owner demand"). It loses on the
missing word: **niche**. R7 names the trigger (demand) but not the law of the entity
(niche). Niche law names the law of the entity (must have a burrow) but the trigger
is delegated to R7. The two are *joint*; citing either alone is the rival's error.
The reason is the conjunction: `BIRTH(ε) ⇒ (∃ burrow(ε)) ∧ (demand(ε) ∈ {NO_NICHE, OWNER})`.

**IF-FALSE:** If `∃ burrow(ε)` is dropped from the conjunction, then a charter can be
born on demand without a burrow, which is the "completeness" trap R7 explicitly
bans (charters/ch-003-maker.md L18: "Forging on speculation, symmetry, or 'for
completeness' (R7)"). Worse: H1 (LB05) loses its empirical anchor because the
*niche* in `niche-constrained` becomes optional; the predicate inside H1 drifts and
the falsifier (LB09) no longer applies because there is no niche to falsify. Two
independent collapses, both named in the records.

**T-COST:** Accepting this reason forbids any charter without a one-sentence burrow
statement in its Identity section. ch-001 L6, ch-002 L7, ch-003 L6, ch-004 L7 all
carry one today; future charters may not.
