# LB01 — Only WF(K) squares enter the grid (C0001, Q0001).

## Anchor
- Quote: `mill/QUOTES.md` Q0001 — *"Only WF(K) squares enter G. This **IS R5 + R6 + R10 combined**."*
- Path: `research/rcvm.md` L63.
- Predicate in claim: `WF(K) ≡ (A ≠ ∅) ∧ (D ∈ Axis) ∧ (W ∈ Wall) ∧ (G = PASS)` (rcvm.md L61).

## FAKE reason (circular, killed)
> *"We only admit squares the K-line proves, because the grid is the K-line grid, and that is how the grid works."*

Killed because: swap "the grid is the K-line grid" → "because it is what it is" → nothing
is lost. Predicate "is" replaces "admits". Fails T-NONCIRC; also fails T-CONTRAST (no
rival named), T-COLLAPSE (no named loss), T-ANCHOR (the predicate "the grid is the K-line
grid" is not in rcvm.md — it is a paraphrase). Illegal kind: **X-CIRCULAR**.

## LEGAL reason (passes T-CONTRAST, T-NONCIRC, T-COLLAPSE, T-ANCHOR, T-COST)

**BECAUSE:** Q0001 (rcvm.md L63) makes the rule *conjunctive* on four independent
predicates: `A ≠ ∅`, `D ∈ Axis`, `W ∈ Wall`, `G = PASS`. Removing any one of them
changes which squares the grid will store; the rule is not a synonym for "the grid
stores things". The conjunction is the load-bearing claim, not the noun "WF(K)".

**INSTEAD-OF:** A claim that "the grid stores results, full stop" — the rival reads
correct on a casual read of mill/ONE_SENTENCE.md L2 if the *only WF(K)* prefix is
dropped, which is exactly why "completeness" / X-PRESTIGE is the trap. (mill/RHETORIC.md
L4 names a sibling: "there are no walls — only attempts spent on the wrong axis", a
heroic framing that carries zero testable content.) The rival loses on the one observable:
without the conjunction, the Curator has no predicate to refuse non-K-line squares (R5).

**IF-FALSE:** If only one of A/D/W/G is required (e.g., drop `W ∈ Wall`), then MIC-23 has
no wall to re-run the gate against, R10 (crossword crossing) loses its "intersects
already-placed entries" test, and the grid becomes writable by any chord. Concretely:
C0010 (niche-constrained agent) loses the `W` it needs to be falsified, so H1 becomes
unfalsifiable — a direct hit on LB05 and LB09.

**T-COST:** Accepting this reason forbids any reformulation of LB01 that drops one of the
four conjuncts. The conjunction is the unit.
