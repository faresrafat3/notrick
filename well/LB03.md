## LEGAL reason

**BECAUSE:** Q0005 is two-condition (arc-protocol.md L10-13): (a) without a kill
predicate the entity *optimizes existing over delivering* (Factorio pattern at the
population level), and (b) without a pre-written condition, *politics* — not evidence —
becomes the termination mechanism. The claim holds only because both conditions are
independent: (a) is about the entity's *internal* incentive shape; (b) is about the
*external* decision procedure. A reason that names only one is missing the joint.

**INSTEAD-OF:** A claim that "we retire charters regularly so mortality is real." The
rival matches normal org practice and reads true on a casual reading. It loses on the
external half (b): regular retirements are still politics-driven unless the kill
condition is pre-registered in writing (the LB02 predicate). The rival is missing the
external-condition leg; it covers (a) procedurally and (b) not at all.

**IF-FALSE:** If (b) is dropped, then H1/LB05's falsifier (≥3 generalist wins → re-forge)
loses its *evidence-detectable* status and becomes a *council-evaluable* status, which
is politics. The Owner becomes the bottleneck because every retirement needs Owner
override (R8). The whole version register stalls because V only advances on Owner
threshold-write (rcvm.md L160), and the Owner is busy arbitrating retirements that the
doctrine was supposed to settle. Real downstream loss: zero-day blocked not by design
(F0006 / F0022) but by retirements-by-fiat.

**T-COST:** Accepting this reason forbids any defense of an entity whose kill condition
was retroactively rewritten. It also forbids any retirement decision that is not
quoted from the pre-registered clause (arc-protocol.md L33 names this exactly: "no
narrative exits; the pre-written condition is quoted").
