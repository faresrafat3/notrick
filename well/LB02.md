# LB02 — Nothing exists in NOTRICK without its death written first (C0004, Q0004).

## Anchor
- Quote: `mill/QUOTES.md` Q0004 — *"**Nothing exists in NOTRICK without its death written first.**"*
- Path: `research/arc-protocol.md` L7.
- Predicate in claim: `OBITUARY pre-registered at birth` (arc-protocol.md L7, ALPHA-7 question 4 at L24-25).

## FAKE reason (killed)
> *"We write the obituary at birth because that's what arc-protocol does, and arc-protocol is the project's boundary protocol."*

Killed because: substituting "boundary protocol" for "what arc-protocol does" → loses
nothing. Fails T-NONCIRC. Also fails T-COLLAPSE (no named downstream loss; "boundary
protocol" is diction, not a falsifier). Illegal kind: **X-CIRCULAR** + **X-AUTHORITY**
(arc-protocol itself is treated as authority without naming its predicates).

## LEGAL reason

**BECAUSE:** arc-protocol.md L24-25 (ALPHA-7 Q4) makes the obituary *evidence-detectable*:
"when it stops being useful" is not an obituary, "it is a shrug." That is the predicate
the claim rides on — `OBITUARY = condition falsifiable by observation`, not the weaker
`OBITUARY = written-at-birth`. The doctrine dies if you accept the weaker predicate
because then nothing can ever be killed by evidence; politics kills it instead (Q0005
names this exactly).

**INSTEAD-OF:** A rival that "we document retirements when entities actually retire" —
i.e., a post-hoc retirement log. That rival reads sensibly and matches normal SRE
practice (arc-protocol.md L15 names SRE post-mortems as an existing). It loses on a single
thing: under the post-hoc rule, the *kill event* can be redefined to spare an entity, which
is the *Factorio pattern at the population level* Q0005 warns against (arc-protocol.md
L10-13). The pre-registered rule cannot be retrofitted; that asymmetry is the load.

**IF-FALSE:** If the obituary is post-hoc or vague ("when it stops being useful"),
H1's falsifier (≥3 generalist wins → re-forge; LB09) becomes contestable: after the third
generalist win, the niche can be defended by rewriting its obituary. LB09 collapses, and
with it, H1's pre-registered nature. Both hypotheses-ledger.md and decision-review.md
L10-11 stop being testable.

**T-COST:** Accepting this reason forbids treating "useful" or "no longer needed" as a
retirement trigger. Only observation-falsifiable clauses count.
